# Pure-gamma curves through `MoxCms` at F32 (2026-11-05)

moxcms's `ToneCurveEvaluatorPureGamma` evaluates a single-value `curv` with pxfm's `dirty_powf`. That returns unrelated values where the power isn't representable, because `dirty_exp2f` writes its exponent without a range check, and it takes a negative base's sign from the exponent's integer part. moxcms reaches it on its extended-range F32 path, which the adapter enables.

The adapter now applies these curves itself. `PureGammaCurve` calls the backend's own evaluator for magnitudes whose power it can represent, so those keep their bits, and `libm` for the rest.

Measured on `main` at `6d9bfa02` against this change. Host r7900x (Ryzen 9 7900X, Zen 4), not measured on Zen 5. moxcms 0.9.1, pxfm 0.1.30. Harness: `pure-gamma-f32/`, which links both versions into one binary. It expects a copy of the old tree with its version changed, as `Cargo.toml.txt` lays out.

## What was wrong, and is now right

Against exact f64 math:

| F32 input, clipping on unless noted | Before | After | Exact |
|---|---|---|---|
| ProPhoto (0.5, 0.5, 0) to sRGB | (1, 1, 0) | (0.6461, 0.5729, 0) | (0.6461, 0.5729, 0) |
| ProPhoto (0.6, 0, 0.3) to sRGB | (1, 0, 1) | (0.8942, 0, 0.3952) | (0.8942, 0, 0.3952) |
| ProPhoto (0, 0.4, 0.4) to sRGB | (0, 1, 1) | (0, 0.5230, 0.4774) | (0, 0.5230, 0.4774) |
| ProPhoto black to sRGB, no clip | about -2.1e9 | 0 | 0 |
| Adobe RGB (1e-20, 0.5, 0.5) to sRGB | (0, 0, 1) | (0, 0.5040, 0.5040) | (0, 0.5040, 0.5040) |
| Gray 1.8 of 0 to gray 2.2 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| sRGB black to gray 2.2 | 4.1e-18 | 0 | 0 |

- **52^3 grid, against exact math:** ProPhoto to Adobe RGB goes from a mean of 4.18 codes and a worst of 255 to a mean of 0.0000 and a worst of 0.009. ProPhoto to sRGB goes from a mean of 4.10 and a worst of 255 to 0.0000 and under 0.001.
- **Against the 8-bit route, which has its own kernels:** ProPhoto to sRGB had 13,254 of 421,824 channels over 2 codes. Now none is over 1.
- **All 2^32 `f32` inputs through the public adapter**, gray gamma to linear and back, on gamma 1, 1.8, 2.2, 2.6, 2.8, 3 and 4: 0 wrong by over 1e-3 on all 14, worst relative error 1.2e-5, odd for every input, NaN stays NaN.

## What didn't change

- **197,105,038 samples on routes outside the fix, 0 differ.** F32 and alpha: P3, BT.2020, a tabled curve, CICP sRGB and PQ, and table profiles as source or target where the target isn't pure gamma, both clip modes. F32 CMYK. Every U8 and U16 route, including all 2^24 inputs for ProPhoto to Adobe RGB, ProPhoto to sRGB and P3 to Adobe RGB. The grid is 52^3 plus every triple of 17 special values: zero, tiny, negative, above 1.
- **Fixed routes, inputs from 1e-4 to 8:** 0 bits changed on ProPhoto and Adobe RGB in both directions, each with clipping on and off, and on gray to gray. Only inputs that were wrong change.

## Limits

- **A table source to a pure-gamma RGB target is only partly fixable here.** The backend fills its grid through the target's curve and interpolates there, so the adapter gives it the curve in parametric form, which keeps a negative's sign, and encodes the negatives afterward. Grid nodes are exact. Between a negative node and a positive one the result mixes the two forms. Every in-gamut value also moves slightly, to `f_powf`: at worst 1.8e-7, on 177,068 of 436,563 samples of `srgb_perceptual.icc` to Adobe RGB.

  Against the sRGB matrix on a target narrower than sRGB, in 8-bit codes:

  | Table | Before | After | Interpolate linear, encode after | Extended range off |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | `srgb_perceptual.icc`, mean / worst | 19.92 / 140.6 | 2.80 / 35.1 | 2.85 / 35.2 | 2.84 / 35.1 |
  | Synthetic, mean / worst | 19.89 / 144.4 | 0.17 / 18.1 | 0.11 / 13.7 | 0.21 / 19.2 |

  `srgb_perceptual.icc` itself differs from that matrix: in-gamut pixels score 3.77 before and after. Interpolating linear scores better on the synthetic table and worse on the real one: 3.87 against 3.77 in gamut on `srgb_perceptual.icc`, and it would move more in-gamut values.
- **Refused, before and after, by the backend:** a table RGB source to a gray target, and a gray profile with a table. There's no output to correct and no route to add from here.
- **Declined by the adapter, so not reachable:** CICP gamma 2.2, gamma 2.8 and SMPTE 428, and `AdobeRgb` or `Gamma22` without ICC, whose transfer has no CICP code.
- **Not covered:** a pure-gamma source to a table target uses the backend's tables, not this evaluator, and is unchanged. Target RGB `curv` routes keep `PureGammaOutput` as before.
